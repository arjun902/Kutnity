Corrections policy.

KUTNITI corrects factual errors clearly, quickly and visibly.

How corrections work

When an error is verified, the article is updated with a visible note explaining what changed, when it changed and why it matters.

Reader submissions

Readers can send correction requests to corrections@kutniti.com with the article link, the disputed claim and supporting evidence.

Update discipline

Visible dates must match structured data. Material updates should change the modified date and preserve public trust.
