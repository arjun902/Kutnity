Transparency

Ownership and funding.

Serious media needs visible ownership, funding and editorial independence disclosures.

Ownership disclosure

Add the legal publisher name, registration details, newsroom address and responsible editor before launch.

Funding model

KUTNITI may earn revenue from advertising, subscriptions, reports, events and sponsorships. Sponsored content must be labeled.

Editorial independence

Advertisers and sponsors should not control editorial conclusions, headlines or publication decisions.
