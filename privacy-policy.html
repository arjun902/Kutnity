Privacy policy.

How KUTNITI should handle newsletter, analytics, account and contact data.

Data collected

Email addresses, contact form details, account information, analytics data and user preferences such as theme settings.

Use of data

Data is used to deliver newsletters, improve reader experience, protect the platform and respond to editorial or business inquiries.

Security

Production should use HTTPS, secure cookies, rate limiting, access control, backups and audit logs.
